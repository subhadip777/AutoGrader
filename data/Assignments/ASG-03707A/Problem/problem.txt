Problem: Sum of Two Integers

Difficulty: Easy
Topic: Input/Output, Arithmetic Operators

Problem Statement

Write a C program that takes two integers, A and B, as input and prints their sum.

Input Format

A single line containing two space-separated integers, A and B.

Output Format

Print a single integer representing the sum of A and B.

Example 1

Input:

10 20

Output:

30
Example 2

Input:

-5 12

Output:

7
Constraints
−10
9
≤A,B≤10
9
